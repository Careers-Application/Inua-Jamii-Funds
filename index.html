About Us

The State Department for Social Protection and Senior Citizen Affairs falls under the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection, established following the re-organization of Government through Executive Order No. 1 of 2023.

Read More ....

Directorate of Social Assistance

Payment of cash transfers to Older Persons, Persons with disabilities and Orphans and Vulnerable Children.

Directorate of Social Development

Mobilizing and empowering individuals, families, groups, persons with disabilities, older persons and other vulnerable groups for growth improved livelihoods.

National Social Protection Secretariat

Facilitating integration, coordination and harmonization of social protection programmes in kenya under a national social protection system.

Directorate of Adult Learning Education

Providing literacy and education to out-of-school youth, adults and older members of society in order to create a well-informed and productive human resource that is self-reliant and who live in dignity, respect and free from abuse.
